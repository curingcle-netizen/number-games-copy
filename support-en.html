Number Games — Help & Support

Number puzzles with a graph-paper feel. The basic games are free to play offline.

Contact: curingcle@gmail.com

Can I play offline?

The basic games in this free release work without an internet connection. An internet connection is required to watch a rewarded ad and receive more help.

Do I have to watch ads?

A banner may appear on game screens, but automatic full-screen ads do not play when a game starts or ends. You can choose a rewarded ad when you want more help. You can keep playing without watching a rewarded ad.

How do I get more help?

Normal undo-enabled boards start with three undos. Completing a rewarded ad adds five to the current board. Shared hints start with three credits, and a completed ad adds three more. Undo is unlimited in One-Stroke Path and Path Twist.

The ad does not play

Rewarded ads may be unavailable because of your network connection or ad inventory. Try again later. Basic gameplay remains available.

My progress or records disappeared

Progress and records are stored on your device. They cannot be restored after you delete the app or clear its data.

Does the app have purchases?

This free release has no in-app purchases and no purchase restoration.
